Open Technical Position

TRAINEE CONTENT ENGINEER

Department: Interior Design Assets & Digital Product Content Structure

The Trainee Content Engineer plays a mission-critical role in bridging digital architectural modeling and real-world interior design catalog structures. You will be responsible for creating, validating, and cataloging rich 3D/2D digital interior assets across enterprise platforms.

01

Asset Data & Metadata Architecture

Build and maintain data, metadata, and content structures for interior design assets.

02

3D/2D Model Integration & Validation

Integrate and validate 3D/2D models, ensuring quality, accuracy, and performance.

03

Product & Material Data Governance

Manage product and material data, including specifications, dimensions, and attributes.

04

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Collaborate with product, engineering, and QA teams to ensure seamless content delivery.

05

Content Consistency Across Platforms

Maintain documentation and ensure content consistency across enterprise platforms.

06

Standards, Workflows & Guidelines

Maintain documentation for content standards, workflows, and guidelines used across multi-region engineering teams.

Candidate Eligibility

Talent Requirements & Essential Qualifications

Review foundational skills and academic criteria for the Content Engineer track.

Educational Background

Candidate must hold one of the following recognized credentials:

  • Diploma / BSC / BE in Mechanical Engineering
  • Degree / Diploma in Design & Interior Architecture
  • Bachelor's or Diploma in related technical disciplines
  • Strong technical modeling & digital drafting aptitude
Technical & Spatial Aptitude

Foundational cognitive and design comprehension:

  • Strong understanding of geometry & spatial visualization
  • Familiarity with parametric modeling & 3D space
  • Attention to dimensional tolerances and metadata
Communication & Analytics

Interpersonal and analytical strengths:

  • Excellent communication and analytical skills
  • Coordinating with product & QA teams
  • Problem-solving mindset and high accuracy in review
Productivity & Data Tools

Daily operational software tools:

  • Knowledge of MS Excel and other productivity tools
  • Working familiarity with metadata structures & spreadsheets
  • Fast learning curve for enterprise asset authoring systems
