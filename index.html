Open Technical Position

TRAINEE CONTENT ENGINEER

Department: Interior Design Assets & Digital Product Content Structure

The Trainee Content Engineer plays a mission-critical role in bridging digital architectural modeling and real-world interior design catalog structures. You will be responsible for creating, validating, and cataloging rich 3D/2D digital interior assets across Cyncly platforms.

01

Asset Data & Metadata Architecture

Build and maintain data, metadata, and content structures for interior design assets.

02

3D/2D Model Integration & Validation

Integrate and validate 3D/2D models, ensuring quality, accuracy, and performance.

03

Product & Material Data Governance

Manage product and material data, including specifications, dimensions, and attributes.

04

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Collaborate with product, engineering, and QA teams to ensure seamless content delivery.

05

Content Consistency Across Platforms

Maintain documentation and ensure content consistency across Cyncly platforms.

06

Standards, Workflows & Guidelines

Maintain documentation for content standards, workflows, and guidelines used across Cyncly teams.
